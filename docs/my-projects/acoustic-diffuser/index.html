A / MYP DESIGN CYCLE

Inquiring and analyzing

An acoustic wall for MAWJ

Through my design studio, Vorm, I developed this acoustic diffuser wall for MAWJ. The brief brought acoustic design and the character of a listening space together in a 3.8 × 2.6 m wall.

The project needed a coherent relationship between its overall form, its changing surface depths and the components that would be manufactured.

B / MYP DESIGN CYCLE

Developing ideas

Develop the form from a curve

I generated the form from damped-cosine curves, using the changing profile to develop the wall’s depth and rhythm. The curve study provided a repeatable basis for exploring the geometry and translating it into a series of shaped components.

Damped-cosine profile study used to develop the diffuser geometry.

Resolve the digital model

I used Fusion 360 and Shapr3D to develop the wall’s geometry and prepare the design for manufacture. The CAD model connected the overall composition with the individual shaped components.

Apple Vision Pro supported VR/AR review of the design, allowing its scale and spatial presence to be considered alongside the screen-based modelling work.

CAD view of the diffuser wall’s layered geometry.

C / MYP DESIGN CYCLE

Creating the solution

Translate the geometry into manufactured parts

The materials combine acoustic engineered wood with extruded stainless steel. I produced the technical drawings and ready-to-cut CAM files for the shaped wood components and sent them to a factory, where a CNC router turned the digital profiles into physical parts.

The production photograph shows the repeated profiles before assembly, making the connection between the curve studies, CAD model and manufactured geometry visible.

Shaped acoustic engineered wood components stacked during production.

Assemble the wall on site

The installation photograph records the shaped profiles being arranged around the central circular feature. It shows the wall during assembly, with tools and wiring still present before finishing.

On-site assembly: shaped vertical profiles arranged around the central circular feature.

Check the lighting in place

A warm-lighting trial shows the central feature illuminated during installation. The light brings out the changing depths and shadows across the surrounding profiles.

Installation in progress: checking the central light under warm room lighting.

D / MYP DESIGN CYCLE

Evaluating

Review the design through analysis and in context

I used AI-assisted simulation and analysis during design development to explore the geometry and its acoustic intent. The calculator study records the relationship between panel parameters, strip profiles and acoustic targets.

The eight-week project brought these studies together with CAD modelling, VR/AR review and CNC production. The installed-room photograph below shows the completed wall in its listening-space setting.

AI-assisted design calculator showing panel parameters, strip geometry and exploratory acoustic targets.
