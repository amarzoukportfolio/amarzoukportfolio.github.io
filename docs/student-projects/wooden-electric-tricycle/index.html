Wooden electric tricycle.

An electric tricycle with a wooden frame, built by students.

Student · Grade 12

Pine wooden frame · Electronics · Mechanical parts

Process organized using the MYP Design cycle.

A / MYP DESIGN CYCLE

Inquiring and analyzing

Problem & design brief

The student wanted to build an electric tricycle with a wooden frame based on his design. This concept was completely new so he had to study and prototype all mechanical parts and how it works together on a smaller scale first.

C / MYP DESIGN CYCLE

Creating the solution

Manufacturing

The build combines a wooden frame with some mechanical parts combined with reused bicycle parts and an electric drivetrain kit. The building process involved a lot of problem solving skills and creative thinking to reach a successful functional prototype. The student was trained on multiple woodworking machines and tools to safely build and develop his design.

THE FINISHED WORK / RESULT

The result.

After testing his tricycle in a safe environment, the final build was complete and ready for field testing. The design turned out as a success, inspiring more student to design, build, and dream big.

Side view of the wooden electric tricycle

Testing the tricycle around the school track

MY ROLE

How I supported the build.

The student brought strong motivation and enthusiasm to the project. I guided him through the design cycle, teaching the skills needed to build and test safely. He used feedback from testing to revisit and develop his design, ultimately producing a safe, functional prototype.

WHAT THE STUDENT LEARNED

Learning through making.

The student learned to turn an idea into a working prototype through planning, building and testing. He used observations from testing to revisit his design and make improvements, recognizing that the design cycle involves moving between stages. Through this process, he developed practical problem-solving skills and a better understanding of how to work safely and evaluate whether his design fulfilled its purpose.
