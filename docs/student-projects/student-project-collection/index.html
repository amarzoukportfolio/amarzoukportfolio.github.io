Student projects through the years.

A collection of student projects supported during my years at Cairo American College. Different ideas, materials and outcomes, connected by a shared design process and guidance at every stage.

MY ROLE

Guidance at every stage.

I support students throughout the design cycle, helping them turn a brief into a considered solution while developing their own ideas and practical skills. My role is to guide, demonstrate and give feedback, with students taking ownership of their design decisions.

A

Inquiring and analyzing

I help students clarify the need, investigate existing solutions and understand the people they are designing for. Together, we turn their research into a focused design brief.

B

Developing ideas

I guide students as they explore alternatives, sketch and model ideas, and connect their choices to design specifications. Prototypes and digital studies help make their thinking visible.

C

Creating the solution

I support planning, material selection and safe use of tools and machines. Through demonstrations and feedback, I help students work through fabrication and assembly while building independence.

D

Evaluating

I help students test their work against the brief, gather feedback and identify improvements. Reflection connects what they made with what they learned and what they would change next.

Guidance follows the MYP Design cycle, from inquiry and ideas to making, testing and reflection.
